# Public Offer

This document is a project template and must be reviewed before production launch.

## Subject

The service provides digital sleep support flows, reminders, alarms, history, statistics and Premium features inside Telegram.

## Acceptance

A user accepts the offer by pressing the acceptance button or starting paid subscription checkout after reviewing the terms.

## Free and paid access

Free access includes basic scenarios, history and statistics. Premium access includes extended scenarios, deeper analytics, content packs and additional personalization.

## Payment

Payments are processed through the configured billing provider. Local development uses a mock provider and does not charge real money.

## Limitations

The service is not medical software and does not provide diagnosis or treatment.

## Refunds and cancellation

Refund and cancellation rules depend on the payment provider and applicable law.
