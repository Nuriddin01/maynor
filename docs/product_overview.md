# Product overview

Sleep Support Bot is a Telegram-first wellbeing product for people who want a simple, calm and low-friction way to prepare for sleep, recover during the day, run a power nap and record how they slept.

The bot does not diagnose or treat sleep disorders. It gives practical, gentle routines and helps users build awareness through history, check-ins and statistics.

Core value:

- open Telegram
- choose current scenario
- answer a few short questions
- receive a short sleep or recovery protocol
- optionally set an alarm
- complete a wake check-in
- improve future recommendations through feedback

Main modes:

- night sleep preparation
- daytime rest
- power nap 10-20 minutes
- wake check-in
- history
- statistics
- alarm
- settings
- premium
