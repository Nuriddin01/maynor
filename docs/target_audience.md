# Target audience

## Segment 1 - students and young professionals

Pain:

- irregular schedule
- deadlines and mental overload
- phone use before sleep
- lack of consistent recovery habits

Value:

- quick Telegram flow
- short routines
- power nap
- reminders without separate app install

## Segment 2 - office and remote workers

Pain:

- midday fatigue
- stress after work
- difficulty switching off

Value:

- daytime recovery break
- calm night protocol
- weekly insights in Premium

## Segment 3 - people building a sleep habit

Pain:

- hard to keep history
- unclear what actually helps

Value:

- check-ins
- 7/30-day summaries
- personalized audio/content preferences
