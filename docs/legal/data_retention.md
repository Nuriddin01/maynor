# Data retention

## Default retention

- analytics events: 24 months or less
- sleep entries: until user deletes account or retention policy changes
- payments/subscriptions: according to legal/accounting requirements
- audit logs: 24 months or legal requirement
- deleted accounts: personal data removed from product tables

## Deletion

User deletion removes user profile, preferences, entries, recommendations, consents and alarms. Payments may need separate legally required retention with minimization/pseudonymization.

## Logs

Logs must not contain raw tokens, passwords, dismiss codes or unnecessary health context.
