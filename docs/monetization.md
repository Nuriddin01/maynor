# Monetization

## Model

Freemium B2C subscription.

## Free

- basic scenarios
- basic history
- basic statistics
- limited content

## Premium

- advanced scenarios
- advanced analytics
- weekly insights
- content packs
- smarter personalization
- richer audio library
- custom routines
- deeper history
- experiments

## Subscription lifecycle

1. premium screen viewed
2. checkout created
3. payment confirmed
4. subscription trialing or active
5. entitlements granted
6. renewal, grace, cancel or expire

## Upsell principles

Upsell appears after product value is shown, not before core use. No scare tactics and no medical pressure.
