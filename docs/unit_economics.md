# Unit economics

Primary unit: active user.

Key formulas:

- ARPU = revenue / active users
- ARPPU = premium revenue / paying users
- CAC = marketing costs / acquired paying users
- Gross margin = revenue - variable costs
- Contribution margin = revenue - payment fees - variable infrastructure - support cost
- LTV basis = ARPPU * gross margin * average paid lifetime

Early targets:

- activation rate above 40%
- D7 retention above 20%
- free-to-premium conversion above 3-5%
- monthly churn below 8-12%
- payback period below 3 months after paid acquisition starts
