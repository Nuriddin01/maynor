# Analytics KPI

## North Star candidate

Weekly completed helpful sleep sessions.

A session is helpful when the user completes a flow and later rates helpfulness 4 or 5.

## Product KPIs

- activation rate
- completed flows per active user
- wake check-in completion
- recommendation helpfulness
- alarm dismissal success
- D1/D7/D30 retention

## Business KPIs

- premium screen view rate
- free-to-premium conversion
- ARPU
- ARPPU
- churn
- grace recovery rate
- CAC payback basis
